SPATIAL ATMOSPHERES

Light, material and shadow shape how we experience space. This collection explores architecture through atmosphere, bringing together visual storytelling and the quiet details of everyday life. Each project imagines ways of inhabiting places where light changes, surfaces invite touch, and spaces offer room for solitude, encounter and personal creative expression.
