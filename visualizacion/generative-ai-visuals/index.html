GENERATIVE IMAGERY

Generative imagery becomes a space for visual research — a way of exploring possibilities before they are fully defined, and of testing how ideas might inhabit worlds that do not yet exist.
These studies move between architecture, landscape and speculation, using emerging tools to extend existing processes of sketching, modeling and visualization. Rather than seeking finished answers, they explore alternatives, atmospheres and unexpected ways of seeing.
