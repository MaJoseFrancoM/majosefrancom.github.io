NON-PLACES

Passing through places we are expected to remember.

This photographic series explores the transient relationship between people, architecture and the contemporary city. Monuments, infrastructures and public spaces appear not only as destinations, but as settings crossed, observed and consumed through fleeting encounters.

Photographed across Girona, Madrid, Paris and Barcelona, the images focus on thresholds, distant views, anonymous figures and spatial structures that shape our temporary presence within places already saturated with meaning.

Girona · Madrid · Paris · Barcelona

Photography and editing: María José Franco
