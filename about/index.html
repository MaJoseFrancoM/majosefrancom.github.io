Architect · Visual Thinker · Designer

My background spans spatial design, visualization, digital fabrication and learning, woven together by a common thread: a curiosity about the patterns that make complex ideas clearer, more tangible and meaningful.

My practice explores how emerging technologies, guided by sensitivity and critical thinking, can expand creativity and translate complexity into forms that can be seen, understood, used and built upon.
